📋 Race Day Strategy

  • Pre-race (2-3 hrs): 150g carbs — porridge, banana, bagel
  • Swim (2km): Gel 15min before start → ~25g carbs
  • Bike (89.5km): 261g carbs — 3 bars + 6 gels + water
  • Run (20.8km): 75g carbs — 3 gels + aid stations
  • Rate: ~90-100g/hr for 80kg athlete

⚡ Key Rules

  • 🍫 Eat bars EARLY — first one at km 8 while still fresh
  • 💧 Sip water every 10-15 min — 2-3 bottles minimum on bike
  • 🔥 Never chase pace on climbs — hold power, let speed fall
  • ⏱️ Practice T2 eating — train your gut on brick sessions

🏃 Run Strategy

  • km 0-2: Conservative start — shakeout from bike, skip first aid station
  • km 2-5: Settle into 4:30 pace, take gel at km 5
  • km 5-15: Steady effort on rolling coastal terrain
  • km 15-21: Empty the tank — no more fuel needed

💧 Aid Stations

  • Every 2-3km on course
  • Sip water at each — don't skip!
  • Take gels WITH water, never dry
  • Aid station at km 18 is too late to help — skip it
